# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in meta-agent.

## Contribution Principles

- Keep methodology provider-agnostic.
- Use sanitized examples only.
- Do not add private business data or internal links.
- Prefer schemas, templates, and workflows that can apply to many domains.
- Include evidence/risk boundaries for new workflows.

## Good Contributions

- New generic workflow specs.
- Better schemas and validation tools.
- Provider adapter interfaces.
- Sanitized example agents.
- Documentation on MemoryOps, KnowledgeOps, feedback loops, and HITL.

## Avoid

- Domain-specific private logic.
- Real customer data.
- Secrets or credentials.
- Claims that a single observation proves broad performance.
